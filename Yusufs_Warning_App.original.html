    Problem and Zero Hunger relevance

    Hunger arrives slowly, then all at once

    Food shortages build up over months. First the rains fail, then harvests shrink, then market prices climb. By the time families feel it at mealtimes, it is too late to prepare.

    Driest on record

    In Karamoja, June to September 2026 was the driest second half of the rainy season on record. Most areas received about 200 mm less rain than average.

    Source: FEWS NET Uganda Food Security Outlook Update, August 2026

    Crisis to January 2027

    FEWS NET expects Crisis-level food insecurity (IPC Phase 3) to continue across Karamoja through January 2027.

    Source: FEWS NET, August 2026

    600,000 people

    In 2024, all nine Karamoja districts were classified in Crisis. About 600,000 people, 45% of those analysed, faced high levels of acute food insecurity.

    Source: IPC acute food insecurity analysis for Karamoja, June 2024

    Who is affected

    Farming families

    They eat what they grow. A failed season means an empty grain store.

    Herding families in Karamoja

    Drought hits their crops and their animals at the same time.

    Poor households in towns

    They buy all their food. When maize prices double, meals get smaller.

    Children and mothers

    They are the first to suffer from hunger and malnutrition.

    Why an early warning matters

    SDG 2, Zero Hunger, aims to end hunger and make sure everyone has enough safe, nutritious food all year round. Warning early turns a crisis into something a community can plan for.

    Show the working

    The STEM inside the project

    SScience

    Crops need water for photosynthesis and growth. Too little rain at the wrong time means a poor harvest. Less food in markets pushes prices up (supply and demand).

    TTechnology

    A web app built with HTML, CSS and JavaScript. It saves data in the browser, draws its own charts and works offline.

    EEngineering

    We followed the design process: define the problem, design, build, test, then improve. See the version history on the Testing tab.

    MMathematics

    Percentages, averages, percentage change, thresholds and inequalities (is the value above or below the limit?).

    Testing and improvement

    Automatic tests

    Each time this page opens, it runs these test cases through the same scoring code the app uses and checks the answers.

    Did it warn early enough?

    Tested on the data currently loaded. If it is the sample drought, the result shows how the rules behave, not real-world accuracy.

    Improvement we made after testing

    Versions 1 and 2 compared each price with the average of the last 3 months. Testing showed a problem: once prices stayed high, the app went back to Normal while food was still expensive. Version 3 compares with the same month last year, like FEWS NET does.

    How the thresholds change the result

    Number of months at Warning or Crisis for different rain and price limits, with the first warning month in brackets. Our chosen setting is highlighted.

    Stricter limits give fewer false alarms but risk missing a real crisis. Looser limits warn more often. Choosing thresholds is a trade-off.

    Limitations we know about

    • The sample replay data is made up. Real results depend on real data.
    • Only three warning signs. Professional systems also use vegetation from satellites and livestock condition.
    • Data is typed in by hand once a month, so mistakes are possible.
    • Crop condition (Good, Fair, Poor) is a judgement, not a measurement.
    • One region and one market at a time.
    • The app does not send SMS by itself. A person copies and shares the alert.
    Business perspective and sustainability

    Who uses it

    UserWhat they do with it
    Mosque Zakat committeeEnters data each month and prepares food stock when a Warning comes
    School food bank or clubCollects food donations early and shares the alert with families
    Farmer groupsDecide how much grain to store and when to sell
    District and NGO staffSee which months need support and plan distribution

    Most Ugandans still use basic phones, not smartphones (UCC counted 26.7 million feature phones and 18.2 million smartphones in 2024, Daily Monitor), and many rural families get weather news from FM radio (study of rural Uganda). So the app is for coordinators, and families receive the alert by SMS, radio or announcement.

    SMS cost calculator

    Default price: bulk SMS in Uganda is about UGX 28–32 per message for under 10,000 messages (sent.dm price guide, checked January 2025). Ask a provider for a current quote.

    Who pays

    • Free for communities. We do not charge families or mosques to use it.
    • Mosque Sadaqah fund covers SMS costs, which are small next to the cost of food aid.
    • Partners such as an NGO, the district office or a telecom company could sponsor SMS for more households.

    How it keeps going

    • No server, subscription or special equipment to pay for.
    • Free data sources that are updated every month.
    • One trained volunteer can update it in about 15 minutes a month.
    • Each year, students hand it over to the next group in the school club.

    Feeding others and avoiding waste

    "You will plant for seven years consecutively; and what you harvest leave in its spikes, except a little from which you will eat."Surah Yusuf 12:47. Prophet Yusuf (AS) read the warning signs and planned ahead. That is our model.
    "And they give food in spite of love for it to the needy, the orphan, and the captive."Surah Al-Insan 76:8. Feeding others is a mark of the righteous.
    "Eat and drink, but be not excessive. Indeed, He likes not those who commit excess."Surah Al-A'raf 7:31. Avoiding Israf: no waste in good times.
    "He is not a believer whose stomach is filled while his neighbour goes hungry."Hadith, Al-Adab al-Mufrad 112. Our responsibility reaches our neighbours.

    Translations: Sahih International. Check wording with your Islamic studies teacher.
